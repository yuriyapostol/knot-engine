# Reusable resources

This section is reserved for actual definitions and records: `catalogs/`, `representation-types/`, `categories/`, `views/` and `constructions/`. No such registries have been published yet. We do not populate them with invented definitions to fill the navigation.

The [resource design](../../design/shared-resources.md) describes their proposed contracts. The [format examples](../spec/examples/assets/open-rope.json) demonstrate the implemented asset format; they are not a catalog of verified knots.

Resource identifiers are independent of the page navigation and source directory. Moving documentation does not change the `knot-engine` namespace.
